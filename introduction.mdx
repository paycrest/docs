# Welcome to Paycrest

Paycrest is a **decentralized payment routing protocol** for stablecoin-fiat payments in emerging markets. It routes bidirectional conversion orders between stablecoins and local currencies through a distributed network of liquidity providers, with onchain escrow that settles orders automatically without Paycrest ever holding user funds.

Paycrest is built for **wallets, fintechs, exchanges, DeFi protocols, and platforms** that need to move stablecoins into and out of local fiat accounts in high-friction markets. If you're building a product that touches cross-border payments, remittances, payroll, or stablecoin on-ramps and off-ramps, Paycrest is the routing layer.

## The Problem

Cross-border payments, especially in Africa and other emerging markets, remain globally expensive, slow, and fragmented. Sending $200 to Sub-Saharan Africa costs an average of 7.9% in fees, according to the World Bank's [Migration and Development Brief 40](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/06/26/remittances-slowed-in-2023-expected-to-grow-faster-in-2024). For the $54 billion in remittances that flowed into the region in 2023, a large share of that value is lost to intermediaries.

Stablecoins have emerged as the most viable alternative: they move in minutes, cost fractions of a cent on modern L2 chains, and are already seeing massive organic adoption. But there is a critical gap: the **last mile**. Converting between stablecoins and local currency remains fragmented and increasingly contested by platform-specific networks tied to individual stablecoin issuers, each building a walled garden around their own token.

**There is no neutral, open, protocol-level routing layer for stablecoin-fiat conversion. Paycrest is building that layer: issuer-agnostic by design, with no preference for one stablecoin or issuer over another.**

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Order">
    A sender (or an app on their behalf) initiates a payment order through the Gateway Smart Contract, which locks stablecoins in escrow. No funds are held by Paycrest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route">
    The aggregator indexes the order and routes it to the most suitable liquidity provider based on rate, availability, and corridor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fulfill">
    The provider's automated provision node disburses fiat to the recipient's bank account or mobile wallet via a local PSP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete">
    Once payout is verified, the escrowed stablecoins are released to the provider onchain. If the order can't be fulfilled, the sender is automatically refunded.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The on-ramp flow runs the other way: a user deposits fiat with a provider, and the protocol delivers stablecoins to their wallet.
</Note>

## What is Paycrest?

Paycrest is not a payment app. It is infrastructure: an open payment routing protocol that any application can integrate to offer stablecoin on-ramps and off-ramps to its users. It is also not a P2P consumer product, not a card-issuing solution, and not suitable for anonymous flows. All senders and providers are KYB-verified.

The protocol handles the full stack:
- **Order routing**: matching senders with the right provider for each corridor and rate
- **Encrypted message passing**: recipient bank/wallet details transmitted securely
- **Onchain escrow**: neither party needs to trust the other; the contract enforces completion
- **Compliance coordination**: providers own local regulatory obligations, senders own theirs

## Where We're Headed

Paycrest is focused on one thing: making stablecoin-fiat routing in emerging markets reliable enough to be trusted infrastructure.

We want Paycrest to become the default routing path for stablecoin-fiat payments because it is the most reliable, not because anyone is locked in. Every new provider makes the corridors it serves more robust.

## Decentralization Path

Today the **Paycrest Aggregator**, operated by Paycrest, is the neutral middle between senders and independent verified providers. It is non-custodial and works in both directions, on-ramp and off-ramp. The protocol is designed so that multiple independent aggregators can run on it in the future. Starting with one is deliberate: the network proves itself in production before control is distributed.

The protocol is designed to evolve in three phases.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="circle-1" title="Phase 1: Federated Foundation (Current)">
    **How it works today**: The Paycrest Aggregator routes each order to a provider, confirms the payout and coordinates settlement. Every sender and provider is KYB-verified before going live.

    **What this means for you**: You integrate once and work with one accountable team. Funds sit in onchain escrow, never with Paycrest.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="circle-2" title="Phase 2: Attested Settlement (Future)">
    **What changes**: Independent parties confirm each payout, so verification no longer rests with a single aggregator.

    **What this means for you**: Stronger guarantees for senders, because no single party decides whether a payout happened.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="circle-3" title="Phase 3: Permissionless Protocol (Future)">
    **What changes**: Multiple independent aggregators run on the protocol, and any provider that meets the compliance requirements can join without approval from Paycrest.

    **What this means for you**: The routing layer stays neutral. It has no stake in which stablecoin you use, which provider fills your order, or who runs the aggregator.

    **What stays the same in every phase**: non-custodial onchain escrow, the Gateway contract's guarantees, the Paycrest Sender API contract, and KYB verification at the compliance layer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
